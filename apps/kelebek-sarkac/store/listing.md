## English

- **App name (30):** Butterfly Pendulum
- **App Store subtitle (30):** Release it at the perfect time
- **Google Play short description (80):** Release the butterfly from a chaotic pendulum at the perfect moment. One tap.
- **App Store keywords (100):** pendulum,butterfly,timing,one tap,physics,reflex,puzzle,daily,chaos,relaxing,casual
- **Category:** Games > Casual · Secondary: Puzzle
- **Age rating:** 4+ / Everyone — no violence, gambling or user content. Contains ads.

**Description:**

A butterfly rides the tip of a wildly spinning double pendulum. You have one job: tap at the right moment so it flies off and lands on the flower.

Sounds easy? The pendulum is chaotic. A few hundredths of a second decide whether the butterfly lands on the petals or in the grass. When you miss, the game shows exactly how early or late you were and the path that would have landed. You will not stop at "one more try".

• One-tap gameplay
• Every level swings the same way: learn the rhythm, master it
• Wind, swaying flowers and thorny branches make each level harder
• Daily pendulum: everyone plays the same level, keep your streak, share your result
• Colorful wings to unlock with coins
• 7-day gifts, daily quests, a lucky wheel and Golden Road season rewards
• Play offline

Contains in-app purchases: coin packs, the Golden Road season pass and an optional VIP subscription (no ads, daily coins, double coins). Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled.
