Event Horizon · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28 · Developer: RST Games · Contact: [E-POSTA ADRESİ]

This policy applies to the Event Horizon (Olay Ufku) mobile game published by RST Games on Google Play and the App Store. The game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, location permission or contacts.

Your choices

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland an ad consent form is shown on first launch; you can change your choice at any time.
  • On iOS you are asked for permission to track; if you decline, non-personalized ads are shown.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads.
  • You can turn off notifications in the device settings.
  • The No-Ads Pack or VIP removes ads.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Reset progress in the game or uninstall the app to delete all game data on your device. For requests about data held by our ad and purchase providers (access, correction, deletion under GDPR), email [E-POSTA ADRESİ]; we reply within 30 days.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
