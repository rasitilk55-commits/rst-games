A butterfly rides the tip of a wildly spinning double pendulum. You have one job: tap at the right moment so it flies off and lands on the flower.

Sounds easy? The pendulum is chaotic. A fraction of a second decides whether the butterfly lands on the petals or in the grass. When you miss, the game shows exactly how early or late you were and the path that would have landed. You will not stop at "one more try".

★ FEATURES
• One-tap gameplay
• Every level swings the same way on every try: learn the rhythm, master it
• Every level is computer-checked to have a catchable moment
• Wind, swaying flowers and thorny branches make each level harder
• Daily pendulum: everyone plays the same level, keep your streak, share your result
• Slow Motion and Big Flower boosters
• Colorful wings to unlock with coins
• 7-day gifts, daily quests, a free lucky wheel and Golden Road season rewards
• Play offline, in English and Turkish

The game is free to play and contains ads and optional in-app purchases (coin packs, the Golden Road season pass, a no-ads pack and a VIP subscription). Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled; you can cancel any time in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.
